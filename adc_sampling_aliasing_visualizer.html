Aliasing & Frequency Folding

When $f_{in} > f_s/2$, high frequencies fold into the baseband $[0, f_s/2]$ around integer multiples of the sampling frequency:

fapparent = | fin - k · fs |

Higher harmonics follow the exact same folding behavior, distorting the recovered wave in unpredictable ways.
