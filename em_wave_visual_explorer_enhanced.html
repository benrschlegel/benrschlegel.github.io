Electromagnetic Wave Mechanics & Orthogonality

1. Transverse Electromagnetic (TEM)

Uniform plane waves are TEM waves where both electric field ($\vec{E}$) and magnetic field ($\vec{B}$) are strictly orthogonal to each other and to the direction of propagation ($\vec{k} \perp \vec{E} \perp \vec{B}$).

2. Electric Field Polarization ($E_x$)

The electric field oscillates along the $x$-axis as governed by $E_x(z,t) = E_0 \cos(\omega t \pm \beta z - \phi_0)\hat{x}$.

3. Magnetic Field Coupling ($B_y$)

The magnetic field oscillates along the orthogonal $y$-axis in phase with $\vec{E}$, satisfying Maxwell's equations and wave impedance relationships in the medium.
